// Under the hood

Six checkpoints, one verified dossier

Every lead in your console clears all six — read how each one works →

01

ICP calibration

Your context — a URL, a description, or both — is parsed into a real buyer profile before any research starts.

  • Live site read, not a form guess
  • Score threshold you set per run
02

Live signal research

Companies are matched against your ICP using recent, dated events — not a static list bought last quarter.

  • Funding, hires, launches, M&A
  • Excludes companies already tried this run
03

Website verification

Every candidate company's own site is checked live before it's worth spending a LinkedIn lookup on it.

  • Confirms the business is real and active
  • Filters dead domains before enrichment
04

LinkedIn employment check

Candidate decision-makers are cross-referenced against current, live LinkedIn profiles — not a cached database row.

  • Current-employer match required
  • Departed or wrong-department names dropped
05

SDR-ready hooks

Each verified lead ships with the trigger event and an opener that references it — not a blind template.

  • Trigger-to-opener in one step
  • Ready to paste into your sequence
06

Live delivery

Cards fill in on your console as each lead clears verification, and a Google Sheet is ready the moment your target is hit.

  • Stop a run and keep what's verified
  • One-click report export
