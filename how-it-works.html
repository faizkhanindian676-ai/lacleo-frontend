What happens at each checkpoint

Every field you eventually see on a card — company, contact, trigger, hook — is produced at one of these six steps.

01

ICP calibration

Your context — a URL, a description, or both — is parsed into a real buyer profile before any research starts. This is what every later step gets matched against.

  • Live site read, not a form guess
  • Score threshold you set per run
  • Re-used across every round of a run
02

Live signal research

Companies are matched against your ICP using recent, dated events — funding, hiring, launches — not a static list bought last quarter.

  • Funding, hires, launches, M&A
  • Excludes companies already tried this run
  • Each candidate gets an ICP score
03

Website verification

Every candidate company's own site is checked live before it's worth spending a LinkedIn lookup on it — dead or acquired companies get filtered here.

  • Confirms the business is real and active
  • Filters dead domains before enrichment
04

LinkedIn employment check

Candidate decision-makers are cross-referenced against current, live LinkedIn profiles — not a cached database row that might be a year stale.

  • Current-employer match required
  • Departed or wrong-department names dropped
  • Highest-scoring active profile wins ties
05

SDR-ready hooks

Each verified lead ships with the trigger event and an opener that references it directly — not a blind template that ignores the company.

  • Trigger-to-opener in one step
  • Ready to paste into your sequence
06

Live delivery

Cards fill in on your console as each lead clears verification, and a Google Sheet is ready the moment your target is hit — or the moment you stop the run.

  • Stop a run and keep what's verified so far
  • One-click report export to Sheets
  • Delete a run and it's gone from your list
